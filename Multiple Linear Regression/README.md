## Multiple Linear Regression 
Using multiple linear regression to guess the salary of a person by using his/her gender and education in Python.
Data set includes 15 male and 15 female people in private sector to with their gender (1-female, 2-male), highest education and monthly salary anonymously.
(1-no education, 2-primary or elementary school, 3-high school, 4-vocational high school or 2-year degree, 5-under graduate, 6-masters, 7-phd) 
80% of data is used for training and 20% of your data for testing.
